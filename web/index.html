Pair Garage Door (Button 0)

Step 1: Ensure garage door is currently CLOSED.
Step 2: At your Multi-Function Wall Console (do not press ceiling purple button), press and hold the LIGHT button, then press and hold LOCK.
Step 3: Click "Send Light Pairing Signal" below while continuing to hold the wall buttons until lights flash!
