Halo
Pair Door

Pair with Your Opener

Halo acts as a new remote. You have 30 seconds after pressing Learn.

  1. Press and release the purple Learn button on the ceiling motor.
  2. The Learn LED stays on for 30 seconds.
  3. Tap Send. The opener light blinks when it accepts the code.
Pair Light

Pair the Courtesy Light

Light uses remote button 1 and pairs from the wall console.

  1. Make sure the door is closed.
  2. On the wall console, hold Light, then also hold Lock.
  3. While holding both, tap Send. Keep holding until the opener lights flash.

Pair a Wyze Sensor

Halo asks the Wyze bridge to look for new sensors for about a minute.

  1. Tap Start Pairing.
  2. With a pin, press and hold the sensor's reset button until its light blinks (about 5 s).
  3. When it appears below, tap it and choose what it's for.
