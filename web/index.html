Pair Garage Door (Button 0)
Step 1: Walk over to your garage ceiling opener.
Step 2: Press and release the PURPLE learn button. The LED will turn solid ON for 30s.
Step 3: Click "Send Pairing Signal" below. Opener lights will flash twice upon success!
Pair Opener Light (Button 1)
Step 1: Make sure the garage door is CLOSED.
Step 2: Go to your Wall Door Control Panel (do NOT press the ceiling purple button).
Step 3: Press and hold the LIGHT button, and while holding it, also press and hold LOCK.
Step 4: Click "Send Light Pairing Signal" below. Keep holding wall buttons until the lights flash!
