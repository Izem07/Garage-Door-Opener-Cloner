Pair Garage Door (Button 0)
Step 1: Walk over to your Chamberlain ceiling motor unit.
Step 2: Press and release the PURPLE learn button. The LED will turn solid ON for 30 seconds.
Step 3: Click "Send Pairing Signal" below. Opener courtesy light will click and flash twice.
Pair Opener Light (Button 1)
Step 1: Ensure garage door is currently CLOSED.
Step 2: At your Multi-Function Wall Console (do not press ceiling purple button), press and hold LIGHT, then press and hold LOCK.
Step 3: Click "Send Light Pairing Signal" while holding the wall buttons until lights flash.
